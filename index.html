How It Works

THE PROCESS

01

Discovery Call

We hop on a 30-min call to align on your goals, style, and deliverables. No fluff — just clarity on what you need and how I’ll deliver it.

02

Raw Footage Review

You send the raw files. I review everything and map out the full edit before a single cut is made — so we’re aligned before I start.

03

Edit & Revisions

First cut delivered within 48–72 hours. Two rounds of revisions included. Feedback handled fast — no back-and-forth delays.

04

Deliver & Publish

Final files in your preferred format, ready to upload. I can handle scheduling and posting if you need the full hands-off experience.
